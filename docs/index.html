How to use this result

Assumptions and calculation scope

This is a sensitivity calculation, not a demand forecast. Price changes scale sales after discounts; discount rates, VAT treatment and product mix stay fixed. Volume scales quantities and product costs proportionally. Fractional units represent a modelled mix.

Product margin = sales excluding VAT minus product costs. Shipping, payment fees, advertising, overhead and refunds are excluded from this experiment, even when variable costs are declared elsewhere. Required volume compares product margin with the filtered baseline, before the volume assumption.

Experiments stay in this page. They do not change the workbook, recorded sales, CSV exports or executive PDF.

Margin Map v1.2.0 · Calculation scope
Product margin means sales after discounts and VAT, minus declared product costs. After variable costs also adds the shipping customers paid and deducts declared fulfilment, payment fees and advertising, allocated to lines by value. Salaries, rent, software, returns and other overhead are still excluded. These figures are not net profit; refunds require separate reconciliation. This is decision support, not accounting or tax reporting.
Source: editable Excel input or Shopify Admin API · snapshot orders-from-excel.json · all figures are reproducible from raw order data.
